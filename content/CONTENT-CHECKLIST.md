# Content checklist

- Confirm the school has permission to publish or link the resource.
- Prefer the original official source; never copy or rehost copyrighted textbooks.
- Verify title, class, subject, language, year and chapter.
- Provide clear and accurate English metadata.
- Confirm the URL works and uses HTTPS.
- Record source ownership and the verification date.
- Set `downloadAllowed` only when permission is explicit.
- Do not include student names, email, roll number, grades or personal information.
- Mark provisional records as `demo: true`.
